Nov 10, 2024 Engineering

Designing a Pedal-Powered Water Pump

Bridging thermodynamics and practical mechanical design for sustainable rural solutions.

High-Res Image Placeholder

The Problem Statement

Access to clean water in remote areas often relies on manual hand pumps or expensive motorized solutions. As part of my final year project at the University of Rwanda, I wanted to explore a middle ground: a pedal-powered mechanism that leverages the ergonomic efficiency of human leg muscles.

System Architecture

The core concept involves converting rotational kinetic energy from a standard bicycle frame into reciprocating motion required by a piston pump.

Calculating Flow Rate

To calculate the theoretical flow rate, we use a simple Python script mapping RPM to volumetric displacement:

def calculate_flow_rate(rpm, bore, stroke):
    # bore and stroke in meters
    import math
    area = math.pi * (bore / 2)**2
    # Flow rate in cubic meters per minute
    flow_rate = area * stroke * rpm
    # Convert to liters per minute
    return flow_rate * 1000

# Example usage
lpm = calculate_flow_rate(60, 0.05, 0.1)
print(f"Flow rate: {lpm:.2f} L/min")

Conclusion

By blending traditional mechanical principles with modern CAD software, we were able to iterate designs quickly before entering the fabrication phase. The next step is integrating basic IoT sensors to measure actual flow rate against theoretical calculations!
